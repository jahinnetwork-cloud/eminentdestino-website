The service, explained

Give your music video a release of its own.

A finished music video needs a delivery plan as well as a creative vision. Video distribution brings together the master file, artist identity, credits and rights information for review by the destination. EDO helps artists and labels prepare those materials and coordinate a professional video release.

01

Technical preparation

File quality, artwork and metadata need to work together. We review the supplied materials and flag corrections before delivery so avoidable format issues do not become last-minute surprises.

02

Artist and channel coordination

Consistent artist naming and channel information help place a release in the right context. Tell us whether you already have a VEVO presence so the delivery plan reflects your current setup.

03

Rights and release support

Video rights can include the recording, composition and visual material. Clear permissions help support delivery, while reporting and release follow-up provide a route for operational questions.

Your EDO partnership

Why plan your video release with EDO?

EDO connects video delivery with the broader music release. You can discuss the audio release, publishing needs and rights information with the same team rather than organize each part without context. When comparing video partners, look for a clear review process, realistic scheduling and an explanation of what reporting is available.

Let's talk about your project

What to prepare

A few details help us make the first conversation useful.

  • Your final video master and proposed release date.
  • Artist details, title, credits and artwork.
  • Permissions for the recording, composition and visual material.
  • Existing channel links and any previous delivery arrangements.

How it works

  1. Send your final video files and metadata.

  2. We run quality and rights checks, then deliver.

  3. Follow views and revenue in your reports.

Before you start

Good questions. Clear answers.

Explore the details, then talk to us about your own project.

Does delivery guarantee VEVO acceptance?

No. Destination requirements and review still apply. We check the material and discuss the delivery route, but final acceptance remains with the destination.

Can you produce the video too?

Music video production is a separate EDO service. Tell us if you need help from the creative brief through the final edit as well as distribution.

Will a VEVO release guarantee more views?

No view count or promotional placement is guaranteed. A release plan should include your own audience communication and promotion alongside delivery.
